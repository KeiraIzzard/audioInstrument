Welcome to Canopy

A study companion that grows a tree while you focus, then lets you turn that tree into a soundscape.

How it works

  1. Press Start focus. A 25-minute Pomodoro session begins, followed by a 5-minute break. Four sessions grow one full tree.
  2. While you focus, leaves bloom on the branches at random moments.
  3. Click or tap a leaf to start its sound. With a keyboard, Tab to a leaf and press Enter or Space. The sound keeps playing until you click that leaf again.
  4. Click more leaves to layer sounds together into your own soundscape. Some leaves are rarer and carry rarer sounds.
